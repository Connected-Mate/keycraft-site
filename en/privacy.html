Privacy.

Keycraft does not collect what you type. The app and its keyboard extension work on your device, without an account or tracking.

Last updated: 20 October 2026 · Keycraft for iPhone

Who publishes Keycraft?

Keycraft is published by Alexandre Cormeraie, under the name Connected Mate. For questions about privacy, contact alex.connectedmate@gmail.com.

What the app collects

The app and keyboard do not transmit data to the publisher. They contain no advertising, analytics tools, tracking or user account system. They never read your clipboard and do not request microphone access. The keyboard does not require iOS Full Access.

Your text and preferences

  • Keyboard: nearby text needed for typing, capitalization and suggestions is processed temporarily in memory. The extension does not save your text or a history of your keystrokes.
  • Practice note: text entered in the app’s playground is saved locally on your device. Clear note saves an empty note; undo history remains only in memory during the session. Text in the setup screen’s keyboard test is not saved.
  • Preferences: your theme, layout, accent, haptic and animation settings are stored locally. Only the allowed appearance and keyboard settings are shared with the extension, never your practice note or words.
  • Copy and Share: text is written to the system clipboard or sent to the destination you select only when you choose those actions. Keycraft never reads the clipboard.

Device motion

Optional effects use device motion for the garden’s perspective and Play’s reassembly. Motion readings are processed on the device, without being recorded or transmitted. You can disable these effects in Preferences. The app respects the iOS Reduce Motion setting.

Retention and deletion

Your preferences and practice note remain on the device until you change or clear them, or delete the app. Use Clear note in the playground to erase its saved text. Deleting Keycraft removes the app and extension’s local data. Any device backups managed by iOS or iCloud are separate and depend on your Apple settings. We do not have access to those backups.

This website and support

This product website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use audience analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or remotely loaded fonts. Text entered in the website’s typing demo remains in the page, is not saved and disappears when the page reloads.

When you visit, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address and request logs to provide and secure its hosting. Read the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

If you choose to email support, we receive your email address and the content you send. We use them to respond to your request. Please do not include passwords or sensitive typing content. You can ask us to delete your support exchange at the same email address. This optional contact is separate from the keyboard extension.

Your rights and questions

Because we do not receive your typing or local practice note, we cannot read or recover them. You can manage those items directly on your device. To ask a privacy question or make a request concerning a support exchange, email alex.connectedmate@gmail.com. You may also contact your data protection authority, including the CNIL in France.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if Keycraft’s practices change. The date above identifies the current version.

Personal by design.

Your typing stays on your iPhone. So do your preferences. Website visits and optional support emails are separate from the keyboard.
