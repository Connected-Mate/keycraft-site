A LITTLE HELP WITH YOUR KEYS

Hello.
How can we help?

Find your favourite keyboard, adjust its feedback and get to know the little details.

Set up your keyboard

  1. Once you have installed Keycraft on an iPhone running iOS 17 or later, open the app.
  2. Choose a theme and try it in the app’s typing playground.
  3. Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard, then choose Keycraft.
  4. In a supported app, hold the globe key and select Keycraft.

The keyboard does not require Full Access. You activate it yourself in iOS Settings.

Frequently asked questions

Does my app selection also change the keyboard?

Yes. The theme and appearance preferences selected in the app are applied the next time the keyboard opens. Close and reopen the keyboard if needed. A change made directly in the keyboard’s palette stays local until a new selection is published from the app. If sharing preferences is unavailable, the app displays that status and the keyboard remains usable.

Why does Keycraft disappear in some fields?

iOS uses its own keyboard for passwords and certain phone fields. Some apps also choose not to allow third-party keyboards. This is a system restriction, not a permission you need to enable inside Keycraft.

How do I switch between QWERTY, AZERTY and QWERTZ?

Use the layout selector in the app or the keyboard’s appearance palette. Keycraft includes all three layouts. The app interface is currently English, and word suggestions use a small offline English dictionary.

I cannot feel any vibration.

Enable Haptic feedback in Keycraft’s Preferences, select a strength and try Test vibration. Actual output depends on your hardware, iOS settings and the context in which the keyboard runs. Visual key feedback remains available without haptics.

The flowers or aquarium have stopped moving.

Check the flower, tilt, aquarium and signature-interaction settings in Preferences or the keyboard palette. The iOS Reduce Motion setting pauses motion effects. Low Power Mode and device temperature can also reduce rendering work. If a scene was suspended after a memory warning, close and reopen its view.

How do the special effects work?

Several designs have their own named action: Ripple in Aqua, Flow in Glass, Squeeze in Bubble, Recast in Relic, Orbit in Aero, Pulse, Unlock in Vault and Print in Mosaic. Atelier has a light-adjustment dial. Play reassembles when you deliberately shake the iPhone, or when you press Remix. Actual typing targets remain fixed while the artwork moves.

Are dictation, Apple autocorrection and swipe typing included?

Keycraft provides tap typing, Shift and Caps Lock, numbers, symbols, emoji and local English suggestions. It does not reproduce Apple’s autocorrection engine, dictation or swipe typing. You can switch back to Apple’s keyboard using the globe.

How do I move the cursor or delete several letters?

Hold Delete to keep deleting. Drag horizontally on the space bar to move the cursor. A vertical or cancelled space-bar drag does not insert an extra space.

How can I delete my data?

Use Clear note in the app’s playground to erase its saved text. Your preferences and note are local; deleting the app removes its local data. Any iOS backups are managed separately in your Apple settings. The keyboard itself does not keep your typing history.

The keyboard is missing after an update.

Add Keycraft again in Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards. If you used an early development build, the extension identifier changed during development. You do not need to delete the app to activate the keyboard again.
